About

I'm Lisandra, a final-year ICT student at Fontys and a designer based in Eindhoven. I've always been drawn to beautiful things, and design turned out to be the way I could make them myself.

Right now I work as a designer at the company where I did my internship. I build their design system in Figma and create icons, social media visuals and print work. I love the moment a messy idea turns into something clear and visual, and I like being able to code my own designs too.

Outside of design, I'm into photography and anything that brings people together. Human connection is what I enjoy capturing most. Most evenings you'll find me hanging out with friends, eating good food, or on my sofa watching a show.

I'm currently looking for a graduation internship from February 2027, where I can combine UI/UX, branding and communication design.
